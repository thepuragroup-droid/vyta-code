ELIGIBILITY & TERMS

Code eligibility
starts with the terms.

Understand region, eligible items, minimum spend, expiry and code combination restrictions. Learn what a VYTA screenshot review can and cannot confirm.

What to check before relying on a code

A code can appear successfully in one cart and behave differently in another. Use the source terms for the actual code; do not assume that similarly named codes have the same rules.

Common conditions to confirm with the source terms
ConditionWhy it matters
Region and currencyAn observation in one market does not confirm eligibility in another.
Items and variantsExclusions can apply to particular items, bundles or quantities.
Minimum spendThe calculation may depend on the eligible subtotal rather than the full basket.
Customer conditionsFirst-order or account-specific terms require separate confirmation.
Dates and limitsStart times, expiry timezones and usage limits cannot be inferred from the name.
Combining codesAutomatic offers and manually entered codes may follow different combination rules.

What the review record confirms

The record identifies the code visible in a screenshot and the observed status at its stated capture time. Unless terms are explicitly supplied, it does not establish the full eligibility rules or a future expiry.

Marketing eligibility is a separate question

A technically applied code does not establish that a product may lawfully be marketed in a particular jurisdiction. This site does not publish product purchase links or treat a cart result as regulatory authorization.
