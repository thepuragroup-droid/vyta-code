EDITORIAL STANDARDS

A source for
every observation.

Read VYTA Code standards for screenshot evidence, dated observations, corrections, ownership disclosure and publication of review summaries.

Our evidence standard

A positive screenshot result requires a readable code and visible evidence that it was applied in the displayed summary. A promotional banner, filename or code settings page alone is not enough.

What we publish

We publish a limited summary: the code, review status, and screenshot date and time. We do not convert an observed percentage into a promised offer, infer expiry dates or claim a live test took place.

Dates and history

Source screenshots can be older than their upload or review. Capture dates remain attached to the observation, and older records remain distinguishable from newer evidence. An absent timezone remains unconfirmed.

Corrections and conflicts

When a newer record conflicts with an older one, the record needs review. A failure is not suppressed to maintain a positive label. Unsupported or unreadable material does not produce a verification result.

Ownership and independence

VYTA Code is VYTA-owned. It is not an independent review publisher. Read our ownership and affiliate disclosure.

Product and marketing claims

Reviewing whether a code appeared in a cart does not assess product safety, quality, efficacy or lawful marketing eligibility. Product evidence and testing content are outside this site's scope.
