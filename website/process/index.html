THE REVIEW STANDARD

A review you can understand.

Screenshots support a dated observation. They do not establish ongoing availability or product authorization.

01 / CAPTURE

Record the conditions

An authorized team member captures settings or a test cart without placing an order. Capture time, timezone and relevant conditions are recorded.

02 / REVIEW

Read the evidence

A reviewer distinguishes settings from an observed cart result. Screenshots remain private and personal information is redacted before storage.

03 / PUBLISH

Approve a summary

Only an approved summary may be added here after separate marketing-eligibility review. Uploading to Drive does not publish or verify a code.
